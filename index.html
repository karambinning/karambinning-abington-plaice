A chip shop on Landcross Drive. Frying for Abington Vale, Weston Favell, Lings, Lumbertubs and the half of Northampton that knows where the good stuff lives.

The plaice on the door is no accident. We sat down at the bottom of Landcross Drive, lit the range, and called it Abington Plaice — half the postcode, half the fish, all of the joke. Twenty-odd years later and we're still here, still frying, still wrapping it the right way.

The fish comes in proper. The potatoes are cut here, not poured out of a bag. The batter goes on at the last minute so it goes out crisp. We are not a chain. We are not a concept. We are a chip shop, and on the right Friday evening that is everything you could possibly need.

If you've not had it for a while, you've forgotten how good it is. If you have it every week — you already know. Either way: come down, ask for what you fancy, and we'll do you a parcel that gets home hot.

The fryer

No. 1

One range, run hot, scrubbed clean every closing time. Batter goes in fresh; nothing sits about waiting on a hot lamp. If it isn't right we'll do it again.

The chips

No. 2

Whole potatoes, cut on the premises, double-fried the old way. Crisp outside, fluffy in. Salt and vinegar applied by the customer — never the chef.

The room

No. 3

Walk in. Order at the counter. Five minutes in the window watching the cars go up Landcross. Walk out with a hot parcel. That is the whole offer.

House notes

The handful of things worth knowing before you order. Read it once and you're a regular.

  • Cash & card both fine. Contactless on the counter terminal. Cash kept in the float for the Friday rush.
  • Salt & vinegar at the counter. Asked, not assumed. House malt vinegar; non-brewed if you prefer, just say.
  • Allergies — please tell us. Same oil for fish and chips, so we can't promise traces. We'll do our level best for any specific need.
  • Hot wrap, cold drive. If you're going further than ten minutes, ask for the parcel double-wrapped. It travels better.
  • Kids' parcels. Small fish, small chips, ketchup if you must. We don't mind — chip shop is for everyone.
  • Tonight's service. Hours and any holiday closures go on our Facebook first. Quickest way to check before you set off.
